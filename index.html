About Us

“Human capital is the engine that propels organizations to grow, gain competitive advantage and realize profits.”

At Talentica Resource, we understand that a company's greatest asset is its people. We are a leading human resource consulting firm dedicated to helping businesses unlock their workforce needs, working towards excellence in consulting through customer focus, innovation, trust, accountability and transparency.

We are a staffing company operating across IT / Non-IT — Permanent & Contract roles, with specialised expertise in Investment Banking, NBFC, BFSI, Information Technology, Pharma, FinTech and Start-ups, working with organisations across PAN India.

Our philosophy is simple: put client and people first in everything we do. We believe an organisation's success rests on the strength of its people — and our job is to help you build and manage the best teams, with personalised solutions and service that exceeds expectations.
